Before you go

Questions, answered.

Is India a good choice for first-time international visitors?

Absolutely. Your private guide, hand-picked stays and 24/7 support remove the logistics, so you can simply enjoy the journey at your own pace.

What is included in a Royal Skyline Meridian journey?

Your itinerary is tailored around you and can include private transfers, specialist guides, accommodation, selected experiences and round-the-clock support.

How far ahead should I plan my India trip?

For peak season, October through March, we recommend planning four to six months ahead. We can also design exceptional journeys on shorter notice.

What does the $199 booking deposit cover?

It reserves the design process and your preferred journey window. It is non-refundable, but fully adjustable against the final cost of your confirmed itinerary.

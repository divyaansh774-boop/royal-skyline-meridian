Your journey, day by day

Day 1 · Arrive in Jaipur

Private airport welcome, a relaxed transfer to your heritage stay and a gentle orientation with your local host.

Day 2 · Jaipur’s living heritage

Explore Amber Fort early, followed by a craft-focused walk through the old city and a private dinner.

Day 3 · Jaipur to Jodhpur

Travel west through Rajasthan’s open landscapes before arriving in the Blue City.

Day 4 · Jodhpur & the fort

Meander through historic lanes and meet a storyteller at Mehrangarh Fort.

Days 5–7 · Udaipur & the desert

Finish with lakeside calm, palace architecture and a desert evening shaped around your pace.
